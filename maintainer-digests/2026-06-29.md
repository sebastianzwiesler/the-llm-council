`openai/gpt-5.4` / `google/gemini-3.1-pro-preview` / `claude-opus-4-6` "likely do not

README, CHANGELOG, docs, examples, and ~15 references in CLAUDE.md. "Fixing" them is therefore
neither self-contained nor a clean one-run change, and doing it piecemeal (config only) would
*introduce* config-vs-docs drift. It also needs live OpenRouter model-availability
verification this bot cannot conclusively perform. Fails gate criterion 3.

### Latent bugs surfaced by the new tests (reported, NOT fixed — test-only change)
1. **`tool_registry._parse_tool` treats params with a `default` but no `required:` key as
   required** (`tool_registry.py:188`, `required` defaults to `True`). In
   `config/tool_registry.yaml`, `web_search.limit` and `code_analysis.analysis_type` have a
   `default` but no `required`, so they emit into the OpenAI/Anthropic `required` list despite
   being optional-by-intent. Consider inferring `default present ⇒ required=False`. Pinned in
   `test_load_required_defaults_true_when_key_absent`.
2. **`create_agent(unknown_role)` returns the abstract `BaseAgent`, which then raises
   `TypeError` on instantiation** (`base_agent.py:294` — `.get(role, BaseAgent)` fallback is
   unusable; abstract `run`). Consider raising a clear `ValueError` or defaulting to a
   concrete agent. Pinned in `test_unknown_role_falls_back_to_base_agent`.
3. `BaseAgent` concrete `run()` methods remain placeholders (return literal strings). Tests
   pin only the real contract (`AgentResult` role/mode metadata, `passed=False`), not
   aspirational model-call behavior. Relevant to candidate #5.
